{\sffamily\huge\raggedright\textbf{Anvil-E2 Phase 2: Official-Weight Multimodal RTL Evaluation}\par}

{\large Technical Design and Verification Report}\par

16 October 2026 (\allowbreak{}UTC+\allowbreak{}8)\allowbreak{}\par\textcolor{vulcan}{\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}}

\begin{abstract}

Anvil-E2 is a parameterized SystemVerilog embedding accelerator supporting text-only,\allowbreak{} vision-only and joint vision-plus-text execution through a shared external-memory interface. Phase 2 integrates directly decoded official BF16 checkpoint slices into independent floating-point software and signed Q8.24 hardware evaluation paths.


All 29 predetermined official-weight cases passed public-interface RTL simulation with zero output-word mismatches against the integer oracle and exact scratch-write histories. Across these cases,\allowbreak{} minimum cosine similarity was 0.9999999950454138 and maximum absolute component error was 0.000047340989112854,\allowbreak{} exceeding the required 0.999 similarity and meeting the 0.002 error limit. Functional regression,\allowbreak{} mutation sensitivity and generic synthesis also passed.


These measurements establish agreement for explicitly remapped,\allowbreak{} scaled checkpoint subnetworks. They do not establish native HuggingFace full-model equivalence,\allowbreak{} pretrained application accuracy or physical implementation performance.


\end{abstract}

\section{Architecture and design rationale}

The design trades parallel throughput for reusable arithmetic and explicit external-memory materialization. A descriptor controller sequences seven connected processing stages,\allowbreak{} reusing the vision and text engines across layer iterations. A single-outstanding bridge serializes reads and acknowledged writes. Intermediate tensors reside in a documented scratch arena rather than a model-sized internal frame cache. The diagram separates control and memory service from logical tensor dependencies: stage-to-stage arrows represent scratch-mediated dataflow,\allowbreak{} not direct streaming wires. Text-only execution enters fusion without the vision stages; vision-only execution still uses framing and the language-side embedding pipeline. Registered clock ownership isolates inactive stages and arithmetic helpers without altering public handshakes. The implementation and memory contract are defined by E001 and E019.

\section{Behavior and corner cases}

\subsection{Multimodal computation}

Vision executes patch projection,\allowbreak{} position addition,\allowbreak{} RMS-normalized Q/\allowbreak{}K/\allowbreak{}V,\allowbreak{} per-head normalization,\allowbreak{} axial RoPE,\allowbreak{} bidirectional scaled softmax attention,\allowbreak{} output residual and gated MLP. Actual-participant spatial means feed the bridge. Fusion scales frame/\allowbreak{}text embeddings by sqrt(\allowbreak{}TEXT\_\allowbreak{}HIDDEN)\allowbreak{},\allowbreak{} leaves image vectors unscaled and shifts appended positions. Text layers use full attention at zero-based layers 5/\allowbreak{}11/\allowbreak{}17/\allowbreak{}23 and local attention otherwise,\allowbreak{} with abs(\allowbreak{}position difference)\allowbreak{}<SLIDING\_\allowbreak{}WINDOW. RoPE uses the documented vision/\allowbreak{}local/\allowbreak{}full theta values 100/\allowbreak{}10000/\allowbreak{}1000000.

\subsection{Fixed-point arithmetic}

External numerical words have 24 fractional bits; signed 48-bit local activations have 40. Let W=\allowbreak{}2\textasciicircum{}24,\allowbreak{} G=\allowbreak{}2\textasciicircum{}40,\allowbreak{} RN denote signed nearest-even division and C clamp to [\allowbreak{}(\allowbreak{}-2147483648)\allowbreak{}<<16,\allowbreak{}2147483647<<16]\allowbreak{}. Promotion is q<<16; materialization is signed32-saturating RN(\allowbreak{}x,\allowbreak{}2\textasciicircum{}16)\allowbreak{}. Dense projections use exact 128-bit reductions followed by C(\allowbreak{}RN(\allowbreak{}sum,\allowbreak{}W)\allowbreak{})\allowbreak{} for Q40-by-Q24 products. Residuals use C(\allowbreak{}a+\allowbreak{}b)\allowbreak{},\allowbreak{} and guarded products use C(\allowbreak{}RN(\allowbreak{}a*\allowbreak{}b,\allowbreak{}G)\allowbreak{})\allowbreak{}.

\subsection{Normalization and output}

RMS computes D=\allowbreak{}RN(\allowbreak{}sum(\allowbreak{}a*\allowbreak{}a)\allowbreak{},\allowbreak{}count)\allowbreak{}+\allowbreak{}RMS\_\allowbreak{}EPSILON\_\allowbreak{}Q80,\allowbreak{} R=\allowbreak{}floor(\allowbreak{}sqrt(\allowbreak{}D<<64)\allowbreak{})\allowbreak{},\allowbreak{} t=\allowbreak{}RN(\allowbreak{}a<<72,\allowbreak{}R)\allowbreak{},\allowbreak{} and C(\allowbreak{}RN(\allowbreak{}t*\allowbreak{}(\allowbreak{}W+\allowbreak{}w)\allowbreak{},\allowbreak{}W)\allowbreak{})\allowbreak{}; normalization weights therefore multiply by 1+\allowbreak{}weight. L2 instead uses D=\allowbreak{}sum(\allowbreak{}a*\allowbreak{}a)\allowbreak{},\allowbreak{} without epsilon,\allowbreak{} and returns exact zero for zero denominator. Final projected vectors undergo masked mean,\allowbreak{} prefix truncation and only then L2 normalization. Unmasked framing,\allowbreak{} image and prompt tokens participate in pooling.

\subsection{Memory materialization}

Q24 boundaries include patch outputs,\allowbreak{} vision/\allowbreak{}text Q/\allowbreak{}K/\allowbreak{}V,\allowbreak{} layer outputs,\allowbreak{} cached PLE,\allowbreak{} projected soft tokens,\allowbreak{} fused X0 and normalized embeddings. Scratch regions are prefix-summed in order: V0,\allowbreak{}V1; vision Q,\allowbreak{}K,\allowbreak{}V; soft vectors; fused IDs,\allowbreak{}positions,\allowbreak{}masks; X0,\allowbreak{}X1; PLE; text Q,\allowbreak{}K,\allowbreak{}V. Shapes and row-major weight offsets follow E001; unrounded local copies cannot bypass these boundaries.

\subsection{Sequencing and faults}

Descriptor acceptance enters VALIDATE without a memory request. Valid jobs proceed through separate stage wake,\allowbreak{} START and WAIT phases. Commands,\allowbreak{} requests,\allowbreak{} responses and internal results retire on distinct registered boundaries. Final embedding acceptance precedes a separate completion phase; after completion handshake,\allowbreak{} the earliest next descriptor is the following edge. Descriptor error 1 precedes extent error 5; subsequently the first detected error wins. Accepted transactions retire before abort completion,\allowbreak{} and errors produce no embeddings.

\section{Verification methods and measured results}

\subsection{Direct official-weight provenance and independent precision paths}

\textbf{Method.} Standard-library parsing validated complete-file and per-tensor SHA-256,\allowbreak{} BF16 headers,\allowbreak{} shapes,\allowbreak{} bounds,\allowbreak{} interval layout,\allowbreak{} manifest ranges and finite values; supplied JSON coordinates were cross-checked rather than used as forward weights.

\textbf{Result.} Decoded 55 tensors containing 2,\allowbreak{}566,\allowbreak{}786 finite BF16 elements. Software used decoded values with struct-based binary32 operator boundaries and binary64 reductions/\allowbreak{}math; hardware used nearest-even,\allowbreak{} signed32-saturating Q8.24 images.

{\small Evidence: E034,\allowbreak{} E040,\allowbreak{} E066}

\subsection{Complete official matrix and exact RTL agreement}

\textbf{Method.} Icarus Verilog 12.0 compiled actual RTL with -g2012 -Wall; vvp drove public pins. Seed 17 covered A: three modes at dimensions 6/\allowbreak{}4/\allowbreak{}2/\allowbreak{}1; C: three modes at dimension 12 with 16 vision/\allowbreak{}24 text iterations; D: three modes at 768/\allowbreak{}512/\allowbreak{}256/\allowbreak{}128; B: vision and joint at dimension 12.

\textbf{Result.} All 29 cases passed with zero output-word mismatches and exact scratch addresses and per-address write histories. B exercised 20 distinct patches on a 4x5 grid,\allowbreak{} nonzero positions on both axes,\allowbreak{} multiple vision keys and pooling participant counts 9/\allowbreak{}6/\allowbreak{}3/\allowbreak{}2.

{\small Evidence: E040,\allowbreak{} E066}

\subsection{Floating-point similarity and error limits}

\textbf{Method.} Compared RTL words divided by 2\textasciicircum{}24 against independently evaluated software vectors. Required finite nonzero outputs,\allowbreak{} cosine>=\allowbreak{}0.999,\allowbreak{} maximum component error<=\allowbreak{}0.002,\allowbreak{} absolute unit-norm error<=\allowbreak{}0.002 and software/\allowbreak{}RTL norm difference<=\allowbreak{}0.002.

\textbf{Result.} All cases passed. B vision was the minimum-cosine and maximum-component-error case: cosine 0.9999999950454138,\allowbreak{} maximum error 4.7340989112854004e-5,\allowbreak{} MAE 2.4860103925069172e-5,\allowbreak{} RMSE 2.8736116710771375e-5 and vector L2-distance 9.954482831057014e-5. Its RTL/\allowbreak{}software norms were 1.0000000229083967/\allowbreak{}1.0000000086127838. B joint maximum error was 3.948807716369629e-6. Maximum RTL unit-norm error across the matrix was 4.218036320224883e-8.

{\small Evidence: E066}

\subsection{Measured per-mode simulated latency}

\textbf{Method.} Measured descriptor acceptance to first embedding,\allowbreak{} final embedding and completion at a 10 ns clock,\allowbreak{} with always-ready requests/\allowbreak{}outputs and responses one cycle after request acceptance.

\textbf{Result.} C text first/\allowbreak{}final/\allowbreak{}completion: 122580/\allowbreak{}122591/\allowbreak{}122594 cycles; vision: 165122/\allowbreak{}165133/\allowbreak{}165136; joint: 327389/\allowbreak{}327400/\allowbreak{}327403. B vision: 225083/\allowbreak{}225094/\allowbreak{}225097; joint: 284579/\allowbreak{}284590/\allowbreak{}284593. Completion latencies are respectively 1.22594,\allowbreak{} 1.65136,\allowbreak{} 3.27403,\allowbreak{} 2.25097 and 2.84593 ms. The complete 29-row ledger retains all cycle and nanosecond measurements.

{\small Evidence: E066}

\subsection{RMS numerical-contract repair without legacy regression}

\textbf{Method.} Parameter-aware expectations checked exact epsilon conversion,\allowbreak{} legacy default,\allowbreak{} wrapper propagation and L2 independence,\allowbreak{} followed by the complete functional regression.

\textbf{Result.} The previous multi-patch failure had exact integer agreement but cosine 0.9913286747181946 and maximum error 0.06919039785861969: the legacy epsilon differed from official 1e-6. Selecting the admitted Q80 epsilon resolved the measured discrepancy without changing software epsilon or fixtures. Thirty L2 vectors were identical across epsilon selections; the legacy default and synthetic regression passed.
